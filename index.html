01 · Profile & Background

About Me

I am a first-semester undergraduate pursuing a Bachelor of Technology in Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE Core) at Lovely Professional University, supported by the LPUNEST Scholarship.

My academic focus centres on developing solid fundamentals in electronics, communication systems, programming, and engineering mathematics. Alongside my coursework, I am keenly interested in semiconductors, embedded systems, and the Internet of Things (IoT), and I enjoy applying classroom concepts by building practical technical projects.

Outside laboratory and coding work, I value clear communication, collaborative teamwork, and active leadership across university clubs and community initiatives. In my personal time, I enjoy book writing and representing Lovely Professional University on the volleyball team.
